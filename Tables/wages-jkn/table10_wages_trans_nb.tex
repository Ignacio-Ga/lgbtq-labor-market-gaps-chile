Columns (1)--(2) use employed individuals aged 18--65 with the corresponding wage measure observed. Columns (3)--(4) restrict the sample to ages 25--65. Columns (5)--(6) restrict it to dependent (salaried) workers aged 18--65, excluding employers, self-employed workers, armed forces personnel, and unpaid family workers. Columns (7)--(8) apply Heckman's two-step correction using one common employment-selection probit estimated over the population aged 18--65; unemployed and inactive respondents are coded as unselected, and the same estimated inverse Mills ratio enters both wage equations. Non-binary and transgender status are mutually exclusive, and the residual gender-minority category is excluded from this disaggregated analysis. All models jointly include explicit transgender status, non-binary status, and sexual orientation, interacted with AFAB. Reported gender contrasts are survey-weighted averages over the observed sexual-orientation distribution in each estimation sample. Unclassifiable SOGI responses are excluded; other sexual orientation is retained. The wage equations control for years of schooling, age, age squared, Indigenous background, disability status, immigrant status, rural residence, and marital-status categories, as well as region and year fixed effects. The selection equation additionally includes non-labor household income per capita in levels (ypchtotcor minus ypchtrabcor) and the household child-count variable (men11c). Missing or invalid income sources are not imputed as zero; valid negative differences are retained. R-squared is the weighted coefficient of determination for each wage equation. Estimation incorporates sampling weights, strata, and primary sampling units. Standard errors are based on 24,574 stratified delete-one-PSU jackknife replicate weights (JKn).
