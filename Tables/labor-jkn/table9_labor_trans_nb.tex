Column (8) uses eligible employed respondents from the participation sample with classifiable reconstructed informality. Columns (1)--(6) and (8) use linear probability models. Entries are survey-weighted average contrasts in probability units, without multiplication by 100. Column (7) uses a linear model of log(weekly hours times 4.33) and reports differences in log points. All models jointly include AFAB interacted with explicit transgender status, non-binary status and sexual orientation. Non-binary status has priority over transgender status; the two categories are mutually exclusive. The residual GM category is excluded. Controls are age, age squared, schooling, rural residence, separate marital-status indicators (ecivil 1--8), the household child-count variable (men11c), disability, immigrant status, Indigenous background, non-labor household income, and region and year fixed effects. Unclassifiable gender and sexual-orientation responses are excluded. Other sexual orientation remains included. Contrasts average over each estimation sample, retaining observed sexual orientation and controls. Column (6) equals one when at least 50 percent of weighted employment in the 3-digit CIUO-08 occupation is female (sexo=2). The fixed classification pools employed respondents aged 18--65 from the complete CASEN 2022 and 2024 surveys using expr weights; regression weights remain the SOGI module weights. Unclassifiable occupations are excluded only from column (6). Classification uncertainty is not propagated into regression standard errors. Non-labor household income per capita is ypchtotcor minus ypchtrabcor, in levels. Both sources are already per capita; missing or invalid sources are not imputed as zero. Valid negative differences are retained. Estimation accounts for sampling weights, strata and primary sampling units; stratified delete-one-PSU jackknife (JKn) covariance is used for coefficients and their linear contrasts. R-squared is the weighted coefficient of determination computed on each estimation sample. The full JKn design contains 24,574 replicate weights. Informality follows the operational algorithm in MDSF (2026), Technical Note 7, for CASEN 2022 and 2024. Boleta rule: literal.
