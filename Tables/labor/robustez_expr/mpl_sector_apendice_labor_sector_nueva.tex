\textit{Note:} Weighting sensitivity exercise: regressions and average contrasts use the full-survey expansion factor (expr), retaining the original SOGI eligibility rules, controls, and outcome definitions. This reweighting does not turn the SOGI subsample into the full CASEN sample. Authors' calculations using CASEN 2022 and 2024. Entries are original regression coefficients, with survey-design standard errors in parentheses. Binary outcomes use linear probability models; coefficients are in probability units, without multiplication by 100. Monthly hours use a linear model of log(weekly hours times 4.33). Employment in the population and participation include inactive respondents; employment among participants excludes them. Other outcomes are conditional on employment. All models jointly include AFAB, gender-minority status, sexual orientation, and their specified interactions. Main effects are conditional on the reference categories and should not be confused with the average contrasts in the main tables. Region fixed effects are included in all models but omitted from the table. Marital-status category 1 is the reference; categories 2--4 retain their CASEN codes. Non-labor income per capita is ypchtotcor minus ypchtrabcor, in Chilean pesos and in levels. Very small coefficients and standard errors use scientific notation. R-squared is the weighted coefficient of determination for linear models; it is not reported for probit models. Stars use the p-values from each model's coefficient summary. The indicator equals one when at least 50 percent of weighted employment in the 3-digit CIUO-08 occupation is female (sexo=2). The fixed classification pools CASEN 2022 and 2024, ages 18--65, using full-survey expr weights. Regression weights use expr for this sensitivity exercise. Occupation labels are treated as fixed; classification uncertainty is not propagated into regression standard errors.
