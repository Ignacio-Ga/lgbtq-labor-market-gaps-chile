\textit{Note:} Authors' calculations using CASEN 2022 and 2024. Entries are original regression coefficients, with survey-design standard errors in parentheses. Binary outcomes use probit models; coefficients refer to the latent index and are not marginal effects or probability differences. Monthly hours use a linear model of log(weekly hours times 4.33). Employment in the population and participation include inactive respondents; employment among participants excludes them. Other outcomes are conditional on employment. All models jointly include AFAB, gender-minority status, sexual orientation, and their specified interactions. Main effects are conditional on the reference categories and should not be confused with the average contrasts in the main tables. Region fixed effects are included in all models but omitted from the table. Marital-status category 1 is the reference; categories 2--4 retain their CASEN codes. Non-labor income per capita is ypchtotcor minus ypchtrabcor, in Chilean pesos and in levels. Very small coefficients and standard errors use scientific notation. R-squared is the weighted coefficient of determination for linear models; it is not reported for probit models. Stars use the p-values from each model's coefficient summary.
